Now Playing Brickman

A little touchscreen that shows what's playing, with album art, playback controls, a clock screensaver and a new theme every month.

Installing needs Chrome or Edge on a computer (Web Serial isn't available in this browser). Installing needs this page to be opened over HTTPS.

Plug in your display, click Install and pick its USB port.

What you need

  • An ESP32-2432S028R “Cheap Yellow Display” (2.8″, 320×240, resistive touch). Boards with two USB ports or an ST7789 screen are a different variant and may show wrong colors.
  • A USB cable that carries data (some charging cables don't).
  • Chrome or Edge on a Mac, Windows or Linux computer.

Updates

Once installed, the display checks for new versions on its own and installs them while the screensaver is up, including each month's new theme. Pick themes by holding the album art for two seconds.

Troubleshooting

  • No port shows up: try another cable, and on Windows install the CH340 USB driver.
  • It won't connect: hold the board's BOOT button while clicking Install, and release it once the progress bar starts.
